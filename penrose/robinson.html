Explorer Help

A quick guide to the Robinson triangle explorer.

Move Around

  • Scroll to zoom in and out around the cursor.
  • Drag the canvas to pan around the tiling.
  • Double-click a triangle to subdivide it by the current double-click step count.

Initial Tilings

  • Choose a seed in the Initial Tiling dropdown to start from a sun, a rhombus, or a single triangle.
  • The subdivision slider regenerates the current seed at the chosen level.
  • Manual edits switch the page to the Custom seed so your current tiling becomes the working base.

Load From P3

  • Load P3 Tiling imports JSON exported from the Penrose P3 explorer.
  • The imported half-tiles are converted into Robinson triangles for subdivision and viewing here.

Export

  • Export lets you download the current triangle layout as .tikz, .pdf, or .svg.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + S save tiling as `.json`.
  • Ctrl + L load a `.json` tiling from disk.
  • Ctrl + E open export — download as `.tikz`, `.pdf`, or `.svg`.
  • Ctrl + Z / Cmd + Z undo.
  • Ctrl + Y redo.
