How to Play

Penrose pieces fall into the well. Early levels are about shape matching, then vertex colours and arrows matter too.

  • Move with Left and Right.
  • Rotate CCW with Z or Up.
  • Rotate CW with X.
  • Soft drop with Down.
  • Auto drop happens when a snap preview stays visible for 0.5 seconds.
  • Pause with P or by clicking the top bar.
  • Goal is shown in the top bar as completed vertices versus the current level target.
